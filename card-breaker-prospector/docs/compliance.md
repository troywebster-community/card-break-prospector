# Research and outreach safeguards

- Collect only public business contact information clearly shared for business inquiries. Do not search data breaches, scraped private databases or personal details.
- Do not guess email patterns, claim inbox validation without testing, or associate a person's private email with a store based only on name similarity.
- Respect access restrictions, rate limits, robots and service terms. Prefer approved APIs and normal browser research. Do not bypass account access or anti-bot protections.
- This project does not send unsolicited emails or texts. Export is for manual review and lawful sales operations.
- Before marketing email campaigns, check CAN-SPAM compliance, opt-outs and suppression lists. Before promotional text messages, confirm appropriate consent and applicable TCPA, state laws and platform rules.
- Retain source and review dates, minimize collected data, avoid sensitive personal details and allow deletion/correction workflows.
