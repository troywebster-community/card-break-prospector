# Priority scoring (0-100)

Reproducible heuristic, not predictive revenue probability. Missing evidence yields zero for that component (not a negative claim).

- Dated activity: <=14 days = 25; <=45 days = 18; <=90 days = 8; older / no dated evidence = 0.
- Distribution footprint: 10 points per distinct confirmed selling platform (Whatnot, Fanatics Live, eBay Live), capped 20.
- Social discovery: 5 per confirmed Instagram or YouTube presence, capped 10.
- Reach: 10 for a verified audience of >=10,000 on any channel; 5 for >=1,000, else 0. Reach must include its dated source evidence. Never add counts across platforms.
- Repeat-promotion signal: 15 if there is sourced evidence of recurring scheduled breaks or a multi-stream cadence; 5 if there is a single dated upcoming-break promotion; 0 if unproven.
- Contactability: 15 if public business email with exact source URL; 5 if only official contact form exists; 0 otherwise.
- SMS opportunity: 5 if visibly using direct signup or SMS CTA and may be suited to competitive outreach; 5 if a verified promotion funnel has no observed SMS CTA (record SMS status Unknown); 0 otherwise. **Do not infer definitively that a business has no SMS system.**

Maximum: 25+20+10+10+15+15+5=100.

Suggested bands: A >=75; B 55-74; C <55. The calculated number is evidence quality + targeting fit, not promise of purchase intent.
