# Research access and coverage playbook

## Preflight (required)
Check search, web browsing, browser interaction and any authorized API/tool integrations. Report which work, which fail and the exact limitation. Never pretend a blocked platform was inspected. Do not attempt CAPTCHA or login bypass or violate site terms. Web search alone does not guarantee full profile access.

## Discovery funnel
1. Search broadly for breaker businesses using terms such as `sports card breaks`, `case breaks`, `live breaks`, `break schedule`, `sports card ripping`, combined with `whatnot`, `fanatics live`, `ebay live`, `Instagram`, `YouTube` and a city/region or card type. Rotate combinations and search by brand.
2. Look for primary business identity on official websites and publicly reachable storefronts; secondary evidence includes link-in-bio, indexed listings, YouTube video descriptions, business social pages and dated public event/promotion pages.
3. Follow *explicit* cross-links between official pages to associate matching handles. If an indexed search result merely mentions a brand and platform, record it as unconfirmed until another source corroborates. Search-result snippets are leads, not proof of current status.
4. Find publicly advertised business emails via contact/business inquiry pages, About pages, link-in-bio, YouTube video descriptions, event listings and public seller profiles. Record exact URL where the address appears. If address visibility cannot be confirmed, leave blank and use a contact form if available. Never guess patterns, decode private personal information, or treat third-party people-search aggregators as proof.
5. Capture most recent *dated* stream/video/promotion. An upcoming event is not a completed event. Cross-check timezone where specified. Record observed dates and source URLs.
6. Count audiences only when visible from permitted sources, note platform/observed date, never substitute view counts for followers. Unknown is missing, not zero.
7. Detect opt-in SMS only with a specific text signup/keyword/form CTA; maintain Unknown if no evidence.

## Access-tier labels (use in notes and evidence)
- `Direct public page`: original page personally accessed and the claim observed.
- `Official cross-link`: official site/link hub explicitly links the claimed channel.
- `Secondary public source`: credible public page attributes the claimed link/identity; not equivalent to visiting the profile.
- `Search snippet only`: discovery clue, not sufficient for an email, exact follower count or current activity.
- `Blocked/unavailable`: inaccessible; leave unsupported values unknown.

## Output coverage
Report prospect count, contacts with actual publicly observed email, with contact forms only, verified recency, selling-channel presence and audience counts by platform, SMS Yes vs Unknown and count flagged for review. Report platform access limits without treating them as zero/absence.

## Efficient batch strategy
Prioritize breakers with their own websites or link hubs and public promotional video descriptions; this improves contactability without weakening the standards. Sample audit at least five records or 20%, whichever is larger, up to all records. Do not optimize for arbitrary target count at the expense of source reliability.
